Product information · October 11, 2026

A thoughtful review, from open to export.

ShareSafe is a native document privacy app for macOS 14 and later. This guide describes the current app.

1. Open and inspect

Open a PDF, PNG, JPEG, HEIC, or UTF-8 text/Markdown file. PDFs may contain up to 40 pages; the per-file limit is 25 MB. Images and sessions have additional memory limits, and text imports are limited to 1 MB.

For a password-protected PDF, supply its password. For visible forms and annotations, ShareSafe offers to prepare a separate review copy. It cannot open every damaged or unsupported PDF.

2. Make your choices

Review detected emails, phones, payment-card candidates and contextual personal details. Select Redact or Keep. “Redact all” applies to suggestions on the current page. Search filters suggestions; exact-text matching adds matching text as new findings. Draw a rectangle for anything else.

3. Compare the views

Switch between Original and Protected, use page navigation and zoom, and undo or redo decisions. Choose a solid mask color for sensitive details. Blur is a visual effect, not a secure substitute for solid redaction.

4. Save a separate copy

Export prompts for review when needed. Undecided suggestions remain visible if you confirm. Inspect every page first. PDF export creates image-only pages with the masks applied; it does not retain the original text layer, links, attachments or interactive objects. Searchable text, editable forms and digital-signature validity are lost. The original is unchanged.

Images export as fresh PNG files. Text exports replace selected ranges with [REDACTED], or consistent aliases with the AI-context option. Check the output before sharing.

What the receipt means

Mask pixel checks, PDF page count and SHA-256 fingerprint describe the export. They do not certify that every sensitive detail was detected. OCR depends on macOS language support and scan quality; manual review remains essential.

Local processing

The app uses macOS frameworks and has no document-upload or analytics workflow. This does not control your later sharing, backups, operating-system swap or storage. The marketing website and a future payment provider are separate from the document workflow.
