## Recommended audiences (hypotheses, not measured demand)

1. Independent consultants/designers sharing a client brief or example invoice: need a separate copy with chosen details removed.
2. Job seekers and freelancers sharing résumés or portfolios: personal contacts should be deliberate, with easy Keep decisions.
3. Mac users preparing a screenshot or short document for support, public discussion or an AI service: value local preparation before their own later upload.

Regulated legal/medical teams and high-volume batch workflows are not the launch focus. There is no verified certification, enterprise administration, complete multilingual coverage or lossless redacted PDF output.

## Positioning and conversion decisions

“Review before sharing” leads. Five benefits: local processing; suggestions in document context; individual control; manual fallback; a separate checked copy. Real app screenshots replace testimonials or fabricated customer numbers. Calm warm-white surfaces, existing red action color, and sage privacy cues match the Mac application without copying a competitor.

Hero copy creates a recognizable everyday moment, followed immediately by the real workspace. Feature blocks explain Find / Refine / Export; a four-state screenshot gallery demonstrates decisions and a successful receipt. Privacy copy explicitly separates detection, human review, solid masks, blur and rebuilding the output. FAQ handles scans, file limits, output tradeoffs and purchase readiness.

The one-time $2 first-10 / $5 subsequent-purchase model comes from the owner, not competitor research. With no supplied checkout, CTAs point to pricing and clearly disclose purchasing is not open. No fake timer, remaining count, testimonial, rating or live availability signal is used.

## Search intent

Qualitative queries: “redact PDF on Mac” (task + platform), “remove personal information from PDF” (outcome), “offline PDF redaction” (processing preference), “redact scanned PDF” (OCR concern), “redact PDF without subscription” (purchase preference). These phrases reflect competitor headings and the verified product, not estimated traffic.

Use a descriptive title and natural copy, static prerendered content, a product guide, actual screenshot alt text, and SoftwareApplication structured data without unverified Offers or ratings. Keep the private launch preview noindex. Configure canonical URLs and sitemap only after a production domain is selected. Re-check pricing, product claims and legal requirements before public launch.
